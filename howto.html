Frequently asked questions

What kind of guides does this cover?

Practical walkthroughs for using Polymarket — funding an account, placing and managing orders, reading market odds, and understanding how resolution works.

Do I need crypto experience to get started?

No. Most of these guides start from zero and explain the wallet, funding, and trading steps in plain language before getting into strategy.

How do I know when a market has resolved?

Each market's resolution rules are set in its description before it opens, and outcomes are decided against that specific wording once the relevant event has occurred.

Are these guides updated as Polymarket changes?

We revisit guides when the product or rules change meaningfully, but always double-check current details on Polymarket directly before you trade.
